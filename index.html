Puzzle concept 1: Stock Phrases

by Gilead Amit

The answer to each clue represents a stock in your capacious investment portfolio. Some stocks are currently being shorted (the answer must lose its final letter before going in the grid); some are showing good returns (the answers must be inserted in the grid backwards); and the rest are overextended (the answer acquires an additional letter at the end before going in the grid).

Writing out the lost and additional letters in order spells out a message.

Puzzle concept 2: Big Mac Index

by Gilead Amit

This publication is a world-renowned authority on club-sandwich hamburgers, but occasionally even its knowledge fails it. For instance, multiple rows of this crossword conceal essential components of a Big Mac, but not necessarily in the correct order.

Once they all have been found, rearrange those rows so that the components are in the correct order, and all lined up symmetrically in the middle of the grid. (For the purposes of this step, consider the grid to function like a cylinder, with any cells pushed off the right of the grid immediately reappearing on the grid's left.)

If the Big Mac has been constructed correctly a message can be read down the first and last columns of those rows.

Puzzle concept 3: Leader Lines

by Gilead Amit

There are five squares where the letter suggested by the across clue is in conflict with the letter suggested by the down clue. Putting both letters in each square (in the correct order) will reveal a ten letter theme word.

Lines drawn between pairs of letters will highlight additional words of relevance to that theme word.

by Gilead Amit

In order to make some of the answers fit into the grid, the same pair of letters will need to be squeezed into a single square each time it appears. Blacking those squares out once the grid has been completed will draw your attention to phrases that lie between them, of relevance to the puzzle's overall theme.
